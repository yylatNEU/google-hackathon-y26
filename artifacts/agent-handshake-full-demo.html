Opening contrast

Why this is not just a POST

A POST says, “Here is data, give me an answer.” A handshake says, “I represent someone, here is my authority, negotiate within it, and prove what happened.”

POST version

“John has two kids and a peanut allergy. Give him a plan.” Result: one route, no authority model, no counteroffer, no signed receipt.

Handshake version

“I represent John. I can share preferences. I cannot pay, share health data, or accept refunds. ParkPulse, negotiate a better plan and respect those boundaries.”
